### Prompt สร้างโลโก้ (Japanese Minimal — Web Color Tone)

> โทนสีเว็บ: Primary ส้ม #f97316, Sidebar กรมท่า #1e293b, Background ครีมอุ่น #fff7ed

```
Design a single unified logo mark (icon + text integrated together as one
piece) for a premium restaurant called "NOVA Eatery & Bar". Japanese
minimalist style. Warm orange and dark navy color palette.

IMPORTANT: This is a LOGO — the text "NOVA" and "EATERY & BAR" must be
embedded INSIDE the logo design as part of the artwork, not placed
separately below or beside it. The final output should be ONE single
logo mark that includes both the icon and the text integrated together.

=== CONCEPT ===
An incomplete enso circle (zen brushstroke) wraps around the text "NOVA"
— the circle is the frame, and the word is the soul. The enso starts
from the bottom-left with a thick bold brushstroke in dark navy, sweeps
upward and around the top, then breaks open at the top-right where
3 sharp geometric orange rays burst outward like a nova explosion.

The word "NOVA" sits centered inside the enso circle — large, bold,
and confident. It IS the logo. The enso wraps around it protectively
like a plate's rim.

Below "NOVA" (still inside the circle), "EATERY & BAR" in tiny
ultra-light tracking-wide text.

A single minimal fork silhouette replaces the vertical stroke of the
letter "V" in NOVA — the fork's handle forms the left leg of V, and
the tines fan out at the top-right leg. This is subtle and clever —
at first glance it reads as "NOVA", on closer look the V is a fork.

=== LAYOUT (all elements as ONE logo) ===

         ╭── enso brushstroke (navy) ──╮
        ╱                    ✦ ← orange nova rays burst from gap
       │                    ✦
       │      N O V A        ✦
       │          ↑
       │      (V = fork)
       │    EATERY & BAR
        ╲                              │
         ╰─────────────────────────────╯

Everything above is ONE single logo. Not separate pieces.

=== MARK DETAILS ===
- Enso circle: dark navy (#1e293b) brushstroke — thick on the left,
  thinning as it curves up and right, breaking open at ~1 o'clock
- Visible brush texture: dry-brush (kasure) at the tips, ink pooling
  where stroke begins
- 3 nova rays: warm orange (#f97316), sharp and geometric — contrasting
  the organic brushstroke. Rays fan out at slightly different angles
  from the gap in the circle
- The fork-V: same weight as the other letters, navy color. The fork
  tines are minimal (3 tines), slightly splayed. It should read as
  both a V and a fork simultaneously

=== TYPOGRAPHY (inside the circle) ===
- "NOVA": clean geometric sans-serif (Montserrat Medium or Josefin Sans)
  dark navy (#1e293b), wide letter-spacing
- The "O" in NOVA is warm orange (#f97316) — making it pop as an accent
- "EATERY & BAR": much smaller, ultra-light weight, same font,
  muted gray-navy (#64748b), extreme letter-spacing

=== COLOR PALETTE ===
Version 1 — Light background:
- Enso + text: Dark navy (#1e293b)
- Nova rays + "O": Soft peach-orange (#fed7aa) — light and warm,
  contrasts cleanly against navy without visual clashing
- Tagline: Muted (#64748b)
- Background: White (#ffffff)

Version 2 — Dark background:
- Enso + text: Warm cream (#fff7ed)
- Nova rays + "O": Soft peach-orange (#fed7aa)
- Tagline: Soft cream (#e2e8f0)
- Background: Deep navy (#1e293b)

=== STYLE ===
- Japanese minimalism — generous empty space inside the circle
- Wabi-sabi brushstroke meets precise modern typography
- Inspired by: Ensō philosophy, Nobu branding, Kenya Hara, Muji
- Feel: warm, confident, worldly, premium but approachable
- Must work as: app icon, receipt header, storefront sign, favicon

=== COMPOSITION ===
- Square format, centered, generous padding (25%+)
- Show both light and dark versions side by side
- Clean vector rendering with visible brush texture
- Flat color backgrounds only — no mockups

=== DO NOT ===
- Do NOT place text below or beside the icon separately — text MUST
  be inside/integrated with the enso circle as one unified logo
- No cherry blossoms, torii gates, or Asian clichés
- No 3D effects, gradients, or drop shadows
- No realistic food illustrations
- No perfectly closed circle — the gap is essential
- No more than 3 colors per version
- Do not render the fork too literally — it should be a subtle V-fork
  hybrid, not an obvious utensil illustration
```
